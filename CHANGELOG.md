Newest first. Beta changes move to Live when shipped.

## Oct 18, 2026: Beta and Beta 3.0 (11:35 AM, not on live yet)
- Worth A Watch counts one-score games in the second half, not just the last 5 minutes: football within 8 in the 3rd or 4th, basketball within 8 in the final period, hockey and soccer within one goal late, baseball within 2 from the 7th. A 21-20 game with 6:05 left now makes it. Multiview ranks with the same rule. The Close Games tab and Close Game alerts keep the last-5-minutes rule.
- One tag per game, the same in Worth A Watch, Multiview and Also Worth A Look, in this order: Red Zone, Overtime, Upset Alert, Playoffs, Tied / Field Goal Game / One-Score Game (One-Goal Game, One-Goal Match, One-Run Game), Your Team, Top 25 Clash. Multiview tags are now Title Case ("Upset watch" is gone).
- Worth A Watch big cards show AP and CFP ranks.
- One home per game, for YouTube TV people: a game in Your Multiview isn't repeated in Worth A Watch or You Are Looking Live below it. Without YouTube TV, Worth A Watch and You Are Looking Live keep every game.

- Bottom Bar: slim header (Scores, view pill, live count), date strip, a My Games / Live / All switch, and a bottom bar for Scores, Leagues, Search and Settings.
- Your Day: big date header with calendar, search and settings buttons. The date strip hides until you tap the calendar. Ends with Everything Else: every league on today, yours first, one tap in, with a back button to Your Day.
- League Rail: search bar up top, a row of league circles (For You, then your leagues, then the rest on today; red dot = live), and For You groups the rest of the day by start time with finals at the bottom.
- All three open on a For You feed: Your Teams, Worth A Watch, You Are Looking Live, then Up Next for teams not playing today. Golf and tennis show when in progress.
- Simple, Compact and Expanded all work in every layout. Tap the view pill in the header to switch. In For You, Your Teams follows the view you pick, even with Always Expand My Teams on.
- Classic keeps the current beta home, so you can compare.
- Getting around from game, team and league pages: on Bottom Bar the bar stays on screen over those pages (Scores goes home, Search and Settings open right away). On Your Day and League Rail a Home button sits next to the X.
- Open To is back for the new layouts (Settings › Layout & Display): Smart, My Games (Your Day / For You on the other layouts), Live or All. Starts on My Games. Smart opens on Live when any of your games or leagues are on.
- Has its own saved settings (separate from beta and live); teams and leagues are shared. The first time it opens, it brings over your TV & Streaming services, home market, alerts, color, appearance, odds and favorite games from beta (or live). Fixed: TV & Streaming came over empty.
- Home Screen app: iPhone gives a Home Screen web app its own storage, so it opens blank on the Welcome screen. Welcome now has a Have A Setup Code? button. Enter CA and your teams, leagues and TV & Streaming picks load in one tap.
