# Scores: Changelog

Newest first. Beta changes move to Live when shipped.

## Beta 2.0 (at /beta2, separate app) · PARKED Oct 14
Link stays up; no further work. All development continues on the classic beta.

- **Apple Sports-style redesign**: dark gradient, big title, menu pill (Home / My Teams / any league), one glass panel with **Yesterday / Today / Upcoming**.
- Centered scoreboard rows with big condensed scores, records before games, odds or network under the time, stars on your teams.
- **Home** = My Teams, Worth A Watch, then your leagues in order. **My Teams** view adds Up Next. Each league has its own view.
- **Upcoming**: next 3 days by date (collapsible), your teams first.
- **Full-screen game card** tinted with both teams' colors: big scores, goal scorers (hockey/soccer), line score, Watch button, alert bell, share, and Stats / Play-By-Play / Box Score tabs with betting odds, win probability, team stat bars and leaders.
- **Edit screen**: My Leagues with drag handles to reorder, My Teams, More to Follow, and a search bar for teams and leagues.
- **Settings**: Open to, Color (Field Green / Brick Red / Navy), alert options, test alerts.
- **All classic features ported in**: Live Now view (Worth A Watch + **You Are Looking Live** by league), Close Games view, CFB Top 25 and Power 4 views, live counts in the menu, a "live now" shortcut on Home, **Compact layout** option (Sofascore-style rows) in Settings, **calendar date picker** in the menu, collapsible sections, **Follow** buttons on the game card, where-to-watch under live games, tap the title for Home, and the after-midnight live games fix.
- **Fix**: next-game chips no longer show a game that already started.

## In beta (not on live yet)
- **Compact / Expanded button** (Oct 14): next to By Time / By League, one tap flips the scoreboard size.
- **Ranks on every team stat** (Oct 14): before a game, Team Stats shows each team's league rank next to each number (11th, 24th…). The separate League Rankings card is folded into it.
- **Compact or Expanded scoreboard** (Oct 14): Settings → Layout & Display → Scoreboard. Compact is a two-up grid of small score cards (CBS style); Expanded is the full cards. Pinch the scores to switch (in for Compact, out for Expanded). **Always expand my teams** keeps your teams' games full size in Compact.
- **More in the game card** (Oct 14), on the Stats tab:
  - **Season Leaders** before a game (Game Leaders during and after), head to head with player photos.
  - **League Rankings**: scoring and defense per game, with each team's rank in the league.
  - **Last 5 Games** with a toggle for each team: date, opponent, W/L and score.
  - **Standings Overview**: record, away/home, conference, division, last 10, streak and point (or goal) differential.

## Oct 14, 2026: Live (9:20 AM)
- **Team strip matches the game cards** (Oct 14): the chips at the top are now mini game cards: league tag and time (or live clock) on top, both teams with logos stacked like the full cards (scores when live or final, records before), and where it's played, the TV app, or the live situation underneath.
- **Live situation on cards** (Oct 14): live games show the situation where the odds go: down and distance (plus Red zone) in football, bases, outs and count in baseball, and the latest play in basketball, hockey and soccer when ESPN sends it.
- **Auto-update** (Oct 14): the app checks for a newer version when you open it or come back to it, and loads it right away instead of showing a cached copy for up to 10 minutes.
- **Cleaner game cards** (Oct 14): where-to-watch stays on top, odds below. The Gamecast button is gone: tap anywhere on a game to open it.
- **By Time matches By League** (Oct 14): By Time now uses the same game cards (abbreviations, records, odds line, bell, Gamecast), in start-time order with a small league tag on each card.
- **Title case counts** (Oct 14): "2 Games", "3 Live · 8 Total".

## Oct 14, 2026: Live (big update, 8:55 AM)
Everything from the beta shipped to live: the new red look (Navy and Green in Settings), Apple-style game card, Upcoming tab, alerts, drag to reorder teams and leagues, suggested teams, and the rest below. Also fixed during the pre-ship check: the odds card no longer says "Not posted yet" on a live game when ESPN's game details are missing.

- **Reorder your teams** (Oct 14): drag the ☰ handle in Your Teams. Your order sets how teams line up in the top strip, My Teams and Up Next (live games still go first).
- **Suggested teams** (Oct 14): Your Teams suggests teams near the ones you follow (New York, Bay Area, Sacramento and more), then popular picks. One tap to follow.
- **Settings back button** (Oct 14): closing My Teams or My Leagues after opening it from Settings drops you back in Settings, not on the scoreboard.
- **Scroll fix** (Oct 14): popups (Teams, Leagues, Settings, game card) lock the page behind them, so swiping scrolls the list you're in instead of the scores underneath.
- **Settings reorganized** (Oct 14): My Teams and My Leagues up top. **Layout & Display** and **Alerts** are collapsible sections (closed by default, and the app remembers what you leave open). The Alerts header shows how many alerts are on.
- **Tap Scores to go home** (Oct 14): the logo (and the house icon) jumps to today on your home tab, closes anything open and re-expands collapsed sections.
- **Golf on All and Live** (Oct 14): PGA and LPGA leaderboards only show there while a round is being played, and sit below the games. Before and between rounds they stay on the PGA, LPGA and My Leagues tabs.
- **Fix** (Oct 14): next-game time and arena are now double-checked against ESPN's daily scoreboard (the team schedule feed can lag). Game cards show the arena and city under the watch info. Before a game, the odds card says "Not posted yet" instead of disappearing.
- **Clickable next-game chips** (Oct 14): tap a team's Next chip (or its row in Up Next on My Teams) to open that game's card with odds, records and the arena.
- **Next-game chips** (Oct 14): dropped the @ / vs and added where the game is played (e.g. Newark, NJ). Same location shows in the Up Next list on My Teams.
- **Fix** (Oct 14): header icons (home, search, settings, refresh) sit together in one row at the top right again. The new game card's stat bars had accidentally restyled the header. On small phones the live counter shortens to just the dot and number.
- **Fix** (Oct 14): on iPhone Safari the Settings sheet ran under the address bar, hiding the close button. Sheets now fit the visible screen, and Settings has a big **Done** button at the bottom.
- **New game card** (Oct 14): tap Gamecast for a full-screen card in the Apple Sports layout, kept in Scores colors. Team-color header with big scores, logos, records, follow stars, goal scorers (hockey and soccer) and the line score. Big watch button, alert bell and a share button. Three tabs:
  - **Stats**: Betting Odds (spread or puck/run line, total, moneyline), Win Probability chart, Team Stats bars in each team's colors, Leaders.
  - **Play-By-Play**: the last 40 plays, scoring plays highlighted.
  - **Box Score**: player stats for both teams.
- **Betting odds toggle** (Oct 14): tap **Hide** on the odds card, or use Settings → Betting odds. Hides lines on game cards, Worth A Watch and Gamecast. One tap brings them back.
- **Upcoming tab** (Oct 14): the next three days, one collapsible section per day. Your teams first, then your leagues in your order. Also an Open to option.
- **Drag to reorder leagues** (Oct 14): grab the handle in My Leagues instead of tapping arrows.
- **My Teams tab**: today's games for teams you follow (with a count on the tab), then an **Up Next** list with every other team's next game. Edit teams button at the top. Also available under Settings → Open to.
- **Gamecast pill** styled to match the watch button.
- **Red color scheme**: brick red (#A93439) with black, white and a cream background, plus gold (#AC9841) for favorite stars, the selected date, Playoffs tags, the golf leader row and "Next" labels. Red app icon. Settings has a **Color** option: Red, Navy (#07357A with gold and a red live color) or the classic Green. Live scores use #E31837.
- **Alert options** in Settings, in three groups you can mix and match:
  - **My teams**: game start, halftime, close game, overtime, lead changes, final score (W/L).
  - **Games you're watching**: tap the bell on any game (card, Worth A Watch row, or game screen) to get alerts for just that game. Same options.
  - **All games**: close games, overtime, upset alerts, playoff games starting.
- **Test alerts button** in Settings, with notification permission status.
- **Game details screen**: tapping a game opens an in-app screen instead of ESPN Gamecast. Score header, where to watch, line score, win probability chart, leaders, latest plays, and a box score. Refreshes every 30 seconds while the game is live. If ESPN's detail feed can't load, it falls back to opening Gamecast.
- **Settings panel** (gear icon): Open to (Live / All / My Leagues), Layout, Appearance (Auto / Light / Dark), close-game alerts, shortcuts to edit My Teams and My Leagues, link between beta and live. The bell moved in here.
- **You Are Looking Live** section now also shows in By League view on the Live tab.
- **Watch buttons open the app first** on phones (YouTube TV, ESPN, Peacock, Paramount+, Prime Video, Apple TV, Netflix, HBO Max, MLB), with a website fallback. Desktop opens the website.
- **SF Pro fonts** on Apple devices (Apple's system font), Barlow everywhere else.
